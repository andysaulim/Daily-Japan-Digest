Japan's H3 rocket successfully launches Michibiki satellite — first practical-use payload since December failure

Japan successfully launched an H3 rocket carrying a Michibiki (quasi-zenith satellite) positioning satellite, the first H3 mission carrying a practical-use satellite since a launch failure last December, per Japan Times and Jiji Press. The Michibiki constellation underpins precision navigation for both civilian and defense applications across Japan and the Indo-Pacific.

So what: A successful Michibiki launch restores confidence in the H3 platform and advances Japan's independent positioning capability, which has both civilian and SDF command-and-control relevance.
