North Korea says it test-fired submarine-launched cruise missiles Wednesday

North Korea confirmed via state media that it test-fired two types of submarine-launched strategic cruise missiles in the Sea of Japan on Wednesday, claiming the launches posed no danger to neighboring countries; South Korea, the United States, and Japan held trilateral consultations in response. The tests mark the first DPRK underwater-launched missile activity in nearly three years, occurring days after North Korea's October 13 tactical ballistic missile test that Pyongyang described as a 'big success.'
