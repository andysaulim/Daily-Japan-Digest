Japan’s Rapidus is said likely to miss mass production deadline

Japan's government-backed 2nm chipmaker Rapidus has fallen behind schedule on its high-stakes Hokkaido foundry and is said likely to miss its March 2028 mass-production target, according to Bloomberg and the Japan Times.
