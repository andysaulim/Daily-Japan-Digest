Economy-BOJ

BOJ declares victory in hitting price target, signals near-term hikes

The Bank of Japan kept its policy rate at 1.25% and formally declared Japan's inflation trend has reached its 2% goal for the first time in roughly 13 years, while raising its 2027 inflation outlook and signaling near-term rate increases. BOJ Governor Kazuo Ueda stated the bank will adjust policy to stabilize inflation at 2%, though he added the goal is not yet sustainably achieved — a distinction flagged by Nikkei Asia that leaves the pace of normalization deliberately ambiguous.

U.S., South Korea and Japan vow ‘complete’ denuclearization of North Korea

Foreign ministers of the United States, South Korea, and Japan met on the sidelines of the G20 in Atlanta and issued a joint statement reaffirming their 'resolute commitment' to the complete denuclearization of North Korea, following Pyongyang's firing of approximately ten ballistic missiles into the Sea of Japan on October 29-30.
