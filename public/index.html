Scandal-hit agriculture minister Yana announces resignation

Agriculture Minister Kazuo Yana submitted his resignation after weeks of Diet pressure over allegations that he provided free bus rides to voters — a potential election-law violation — and a subsequent remark on road budget spending that drew widespread criticism. His exit, while key agricultural ministry bills remain under parliamentary debate, is the most significant ministerial departure of the Takaichi cabinet to date.

Defense

Russia arrests Japanese journalist on espionage charges

Russia's FSB detained Yuki Imai, a reporter for the Hokkaido Shimbun, in the Sakhalin region and accused him of collecting information on Russia's defence capabilities for Tokyo; Russian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova confirmed the arrest and identified the reporter by name. Japan's government, describing the detention as entirely unexpected, is seeking information and impartial treatment through diplomatic channels and is reported to be summoning the Russian ambassador.

China sends aircraft carrier group into western Pacific

A Chinese aircraft carrier group has entered the western Pacific, with Japan described as watching closely; Beijing characterised the exercises as 'realistic' and said they are 'not directed against any specific country or target.' The deployment follows an established pattern of Chinese carrier transits near the Nansei (Ryukyu) island chain that Japan has been tracking with heightened attention since 2024–2025.
