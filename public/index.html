Japan mulls exception to three non-nuclear principles in revised security strategy

Japan is considering carving out an exception to its longstanding three non-nuclear principles — not possessing, producing, or permitting the introduction of nuclear weapons — as part of the ongoing 2026 review of the Three Strategic Documents, per Kyodo. The move would represent the most significant revision to Japan's nuclear posture since the principles were codified, and arrives as China's Foreign Ministry publicly accuses Tokyo of 'reckless remilitarization.'
