Former Prime Minister Yasuo Fukuda dies at 90

Yasuo Fukuda, Japan's first second-generation prime minister and a proponent of Japan-China engagement who served from September 2007, died at 90, per Japan Times, NHK World, Reuters, and Kyodo. Fukuda chaired the 2008 Toyako G8 Summit and was known for prioritizing dialogue with Beijing; China's Foreign Ministry expressed condolences via Xinhua.
