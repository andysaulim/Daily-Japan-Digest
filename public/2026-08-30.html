Tokyo to build underground missile shelter near Tokyo Station

Japan will convert a car park near Tokyo Station into an underground missile shelter, the government announced, marking a new phase of civil-defense infrastructure investment in the capital. So what: The Tokyo Station shelter signals that Japan's civil-defense buildup is now extending into central urban infrastructure, a development relevant to both the Three Strategic Documents review and domestic public communication on the threat environment.
