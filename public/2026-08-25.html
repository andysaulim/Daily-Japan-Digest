BOJ September rate hike at 80% market probability as underlying inflation gauge holds at 2.3%

Japan's underlying inflation gauge rose 2.3%, and as of Monday afternoon Tokyo traders were pricing approximately 80% probability of a BOJ rate hike at the next policy decision, per a Japan Times report citing a former BOJ official. The BOJ policy rate currently stands at 3.8%, with JGB 10-year yield at 1.50%, as the central bank navigates persistent above-target core inflation against a backdrop of a yen that has lost more than 30% against the dollar over the past five years.

So what: The September BOJ meeting is the immediate decision point; a hike would extend Japan's monetary tightening cycle and has direct implications for yen carry-trade dynamics and US-Japan financial flows.

Japanese lawmakers visit Beijing to mend ties amid deep freeze, fallout from Takaichi's Taiwan remark

A cross-party delegation of Japanese lawmakers traveled to Beijing seeking to ease bilateral tensions, with multiple outlets confirming the visit follows fallout from PM Takaichi's remarks on Taiwan. So what: The delegation's reception in Beijing and any Chinese official statements will indicate whether Beijing is willing to stabilize the relationship despite Takaichi's Taiwan remarks and her August 15-16 Yasukuni-related conduct.
