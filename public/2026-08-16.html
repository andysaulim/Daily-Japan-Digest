Takaichi omits 'remorse' in first WWII memorial address; bows toward Yasukuni from distance

PM Takaichi delivered Japan's 81st WWII end-anniversary address at the Nippon Budokan on August 15 without using the word 'remorse,' a formulation absent from her speech, and bowed toward Yasukuni Shrine from a distance rather than visiting in person, sending a ritual offering instead. Defense Minister Koizumi made a physical Yasukuni visit the same day, and LDP executive Kobayashi also attended the shrine, collectively prompting formal protests from Beijing and Seoul.

So what: Takaichi's formulation on 'remorse' and the division of labor — PM staying away while the defense minister visits — will set the baseline for China-Japan and Korea-Japan diplomatic temperature heading into autumn.

Nine dead in historic Chiba flooding; first-ever highest-level rain warning issued for region

Heavy rain beginning late Thursday flooded more than 1,000 homes in Chiba prefecture, killed nine people, snarled roads and rail services, and triggered landslide warnings and power outages, NHK reported on Sunday. Forecasters issued the highest-level heavy rain warning for the Chiba region for the first time on record.

So what: The Chiba disaster follows the Kumamoto earthquake response PM Takaichi led in early August, compounding domestic pressure and keeping the PM's focus on back-to-back natural disaster management.

Koizumi heads to Canberra Tuesday to meet Marles; Mogami-class frigates and security cooperation on agenda

Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi will travel to Australia this week for a Tuesday meeting with Australian Deputy PM and Defense Minister Richard Marles, with discussions to focus on Australia's acquisition of Japan's Mogami-class frigates and enhanced security cooperation. The visit comes after controversy over Australian PM Albanese's podcast comments about a gift of melons from PM Takaichi, which The Australian characterized as risking the partnership.

So what: The Mogami-class frigate acquisition represents a concrete test of Japan's expanding defense export capability and Reciprocal Access Agreement operationalization with Australia.
