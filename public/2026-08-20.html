Japan July trade deficit quadruples as record imports eclipse fastest export growth since 2022

So what: The deficit trajectory, combined with Brent crude at $93.76, puts additional pressure on Japan's current account and feeds into ongoing JGB yield pressure and BOJ rate-path uncertainty.
