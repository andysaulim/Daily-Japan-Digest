Tokyo plans underground missile shelter near Tokyo Station amid DPRK and China threat

Tokyo's metropolitan government is planning to build an underground missile shelter near Tokyo Station, the Japan Times and Nikkei Asia both report, driven by North Korea's ramped-up ballistic missile testing and China's growing arsenal. The shelter would be among the first hardened civilian missile-protection facilities in the Japanese capital.

So what: The project reflects a concrete shift in Japan's civil-defense posture — a domain long underdeveloped relative to military deterrence — and will require coordination between the metropolitan government, the Cabinet, and MOD under the ongoing Three Strategic Documents review.
