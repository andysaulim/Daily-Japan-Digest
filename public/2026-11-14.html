White House demanding Japan triple host-nation support for U.S. troops

The United States is demanding Japan provide ¥3 trillion over the five years starting in fiscal 2027 under the Host Nation Support program — roughly three times the current five-year total of approximately ¥1 trillion, according to government sources. The demand arrives as Tokyo simultaneously debates capping its own five-year defense plan at ¥60 trillion against a Defense Ministry estimate of ¥70-80 trillion, compressing the fiscal space on both sides of the equation.

Defense

Japan considers setting five-year defense spending plan at ¥60 trillion

The government is weighing a ¥60 trillion ceiling for Japan's next five-year defense spending plan, below the Defense Ministry's own estimate of ¥70 trillion to ¥80 trillion as necessary. The gap between ministry request and government ceiling, if maintained, would represent the largest stated shortfall in Japan's post-2022 defense buildup.
