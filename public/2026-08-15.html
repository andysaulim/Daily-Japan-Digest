Takaichi omits 'remorse' in first WWII memorial address on 81st anniversary

PM Takaichi delivered Japan's August 15 war memorial address without using the word 'remorse' — a term her immediate predecessor Shigeru Ishiba had included — while also incorporating 'Indo-Pacific' as a foreign policy marker; she sent a ritual cash offering to Yasukuni Shrine but did not visit in person. So what: China and South Korea have lodged formal protests; the omission of 'remorse' will be parsed by Beijing, Seoul, and Washington as a signal of Takaichi's historical-memory posture ahead of upcoming bilateral diplomatic engagements.

Tokyo is actively deliberating its response to Putin's first-ever personal visit to Etorofu island in the Japan-claimed Northern Territories, with options including strengthening economic sanctions and temporarily recalling Japan's ambassador to Russia, per Japan Times.
