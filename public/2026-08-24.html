Australia, Japan, US, and Alcoa break ground on gallium project in Western Australia

Australia, Japan, the United States, and Alcoa held a groundbreaking ceremony for a gallium extraction and processing project in Western Australia, marking a concrete trilateral step toward critical-minerals supply-chain diversification. Foreign Policy Japan
Ditching Chinese Rare Earths Is Hard. Just Ask Japan.
Japan's decades of effort to reduce dependence on Chinese rare earths demonstrate that supply-chain diversification is structurally slow and costly, even for a motivated and technologically capable country.
Foreign Policy examines Japan's experience as the longest-running case study in rare-earth decoupling from China, reviewing the 2010 trawler incident and subsequent stockpiling and recycling investments. The piece finds that despite Japan's head start, Chinese processing dominance and cost structures continue to constrain meaningful diversification. The analysis arrives as China's gallium and germanium export controls directly affect Japanese semiconductor and defense manufacturers.
Japan's experience provides a calibration benchmark for US and allied policymakers assessing how quickly the $550B investment framework and multilateral projects like the Western Australia gallium groundbreaking can realistically shift supply-chain dependence.
Asia Policy Point
Can Takaichi Win the Tax Cut Battle?
PM Takaichi's push for a food-tax cut faces resistance from within the ruling LDP and from fiscal realists who see it as populist spending without a credible revenue offset.
Asia Policy Point examines the political arithmetic behind Takaichi's signature food-tax cut proposal, noting LDP internal pushback and the absence of a viable financing mechanism. The piece frames the tax cut as a bid to reverse falling approval ratings, but finds the coalition math uncertain. The analysis is directly relevant given Nikkei Asia's concurrent reporting of a ¥9.3 trillion ($63 billion) fiscal shortfall.
Whether Takaichi can advance the food-tax cut through an LDP caucus skeptical of the fiscal cost will determine the budget trajectory heading into the autumn Diet session and the December cabinet draft budget.
