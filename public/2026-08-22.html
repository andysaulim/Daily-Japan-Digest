Japan to invest additional $940m in homegrown chipmaker Rapidus

Tokyo will inject a further $940 million into Rapidus, the Hokkaido-based 2nm foundry venture, as it presses its domestic semiconductor buildout. The commitment comes as Japan projects a $63 billion fiscal shortfall for its food tax cut and defense buildup.
