China and Japan publicly contradict each other over whether FMs met at ASEAN in Manila

China's MOFA and CGTN reiterated on July 28 that no exchange occurred between Foreign Minister Wang Yi and Japan's foreign minister at the ASEAN ministerial in Manila, directly contradicting Tokyo's account of the meeting. The dispute, reported by ABC News, NHK, and Japan Times, extends a public communication breakdown between Beijing and Tokyo that predates the ASEAN gathering.

So what: The disputed account of contact between the two foreign ministers leaves the Japan-China diplomatic channel uncertain at a moment when bilateral tensions remain elevated over military drills, Taiwan remarks, and the SDF embassy-intrusion incident.

M7.1 earthquake strikes Kumamoto prefecture, several injured

A magnitude 7.1 earthquake struck Kumamoto prefecture in southwestern Japan on July 28, injuring several people, per Mainichi and Japan Times. Kumamoto hosts the TSMC Japan (JASM) semiconductor fabrication complex, making any infrastructure disruption directly relevant to Japan's chip supply chain.

So what: MOD and local authorities will assess structural damage; any disruption to the JASM fab complex in Kumamoto would bear directly on Japan's semiconductor economic-security commitments under the US-Japan strategic investment framework.

Mainichi; Japan Times; Nikkei Asia; Jiji Press
Politics-Diet

Inter-party consumption tax cut panel collapses without consensus; Takaichi vows bill submission

Japan's inter-party panel tasked with hammering out a consumption tax cut agreement gave up on reaching consensus, per Jiji Press, despite PM Takaichi's stated deadline of early August for talks to conclude. Takaichi immediately pledged to submit a consumption tax cut bill to the Diet promptly and separately stressed readiness to decide on a food tax cut.

So what: The panel's failure forces the Takaichi cabinet to either push a unilateral bill through the Diet — testing coalition arithmetic — or delay further, compounding political pressure as cabinet approval ratings track downward.
