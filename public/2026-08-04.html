US and Japan confirm rare joint yen intervention; Bessent signals readiness to repeat

The US and Japan issued a joint statement confirming coordinated yen-buying intervention — with the US executing euro sales to buy yen in an unusual cross-currency operation — and Treasury Secretary Bessent publicly signaled readiness to repeat the action while calling on the Federal Reserve for a larger backstop. Japan is estimated to have purchased approximately ¥5 trillion on August 1; USD/JPY fell roughly 3.82% to 157.61.

So what: Bessent's public call on the Fed for expanded swap or repo backstop introduces a new institutional dimension to the intervention framework that the BOJ and markets will need to price.

Defense

Japan's 2026 Defense White Paper frames military buildup as economic path, warns on China-Russia-NK links

Japan's 2026 Defense White Paper calls for a stronger defense industrial base and expanded arms exports, and warns explicitly of deepening military ties among China, Russia, and North Korea. The report frames the defense buildup as a driver of economic prosperity and calls for adapting to 'new ways of warfare.'

So what: The White Paper's economic-security framing aligns the defense buildup narrative with PM Takaichi's fiscal push and sets up the upcoming Three Strategic Documents 2026 review.

Pattern: Japan's December 2022 National Security Strategy first committed to counterstrike capability and a 2% GDP defense-spending target, establishing the policy baseline this White Paper operationalizes.
