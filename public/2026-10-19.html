Takaichi Cabinet approval rate hits record low at 49.1%: Kyodo poll

The Kyodo News poll records the first sub-50% approval reading for the Sanae Takaichi cabinet since her October 2025 inauguration, with 49.1% support — a record low. BOJ Governor Kazuo Ueda stated Monday that Japan's underlying inflation is 'quite close' to the 2% price stability target and identified AI-driven demand as an additional upward price pressure. Ueda kept the JGB bond-purchase tapering stance unchanged and indicated further rate hikes remain on the table, with the yen holding near 158.00 following his remarks.

Japan seeks territorial solution with Russia 70 yrs after joint declaration

The Japanese government vowed Monday to continue seeking a territorial resolution with Russia on the 70th anniversary of the 1956 Japan-Soviet Joint Declaration, despite bilateral relations at their lowest point in decades. Russia has suspended peace-treaty talks in retaliation for Japanese sanctions and has asserted the territorial dispute is settled, while its FSB continues to hold Hokkaido Shimbun reporter Yuki Imai in Sakhalin.

Japan leads mission to make first return trip to Mars

Japan's JAXA MMX (Martian Moons eXploration) spacecraft is set to launch early Tuesday morning Japan time on a five-year round-trip mission to collect samples from Phobos, the first such return mission to a Mars moon in history. The ¥55.3 billion mission will be watched for potential biosignature implications from material originating in Mars's vicinity.
