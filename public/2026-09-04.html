Defense Minister Koizumi rules out recruiting foreign nationals for SDF

Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi stated that his ministry's top priority "must be the people who are currently serving," flatly rejecting proposals to allow foreign national recruitment into the Self-Defense Forces. The statement closes a debate that had emerged amid SDF personnel shortfalls.

So what: The ruling forecloses a near-term structural fix to SDF manpower gaps, keeping pressure on MOD to find alternative solutions — including expanded AI and unmanned systems — which PM Takaichi separately pledged to accelerate at the Senior Commanders' Conference.
