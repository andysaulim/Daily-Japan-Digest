Top Stories
Defense

Japan, UK, Italy eye Japanese factory for GCAP next-generation fighter production

Japan, Britain, and Italy are discussing locating a production facility for the GCAP next-generation stealth fighter jet in Japan, per Kyodo News and Mainichi reporting. The three countries launched the Global Combat Air Programme in December 2022 as a trilateral defense-industrial partnership; siting production in Japan would mark a significant expansion of Japan's defense manufacturing role.

So what: A decision to build GCAP in Japan would represent the first co-production of a Western-partnered combat aircraft on Japanese soil and accelerate Japan's defense-industrial integration with NATO-aligned partners.

Kyodo News; Mainichi
Indo-Pacific

US, Japan, Philippines complete South China Sea trilateral maritime exercises

US, Japanese, and Philippine forces completed a Multilateral Maritime Cooperative Activity in the South China Sea, per INDOPACOM, Bloomberg, and Reuters. The exercise follows a week of elevated Sino-Russian naval activity near Japan's claimed EEZ near Okinotorishima and a China Coast Guard interception of a Japanese patrol vessel off eastern Taiwan.

So what: The trilateral exercise directly reinforces the US-Japan-Philippines maritime security framework at a moment of heightened Chinese and Russian naval pressure on Japan's maritime perimeter.

Bloomberg; Reuters; INDOPACOM
Defense

Japan launches national intelligence bureau and oversight body July 31

Japan will launch a new national intelligence bureau and an accompanying intelligence oversight body on July 31, per UPI and the Washington Times. The new structures represent a formal reorganization of Japan's intelligence architecture.

So what: The July 31 launch date is a fixed near-term milestone to watch for details on the bureau's mandate, staffing, and relationship to existing intelligence structures including the National Security Secretariat.

UPI; Washington Times
