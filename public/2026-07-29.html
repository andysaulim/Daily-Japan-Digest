Kumamoto M7.1 earthquake kills 13; rescuers dig through collapsed mall

A M7.1 earthquake struck Kumamoto Prefecture on July 29, killing at least 13 people and trapping others in a collapsed shopping mall where a secondary blast occurred; PM Takaichi convened an emergency headquarters and called for overnight rescue efforts. The Kyushu shinkansen was suspended and international offers of support arrived from the US, China, and Taiwan, with TSMC confirming its Kumamoto plant was undamaged.

So what: The earthquake response will consume the PM's political bandwidth this week and test the government's crisis-management credibility against the backdrop of already-declining approval ratings.

Pattern: Seismologists attribute the quake to a fault segment that did not rupture in the April 2016 Kumamoto earthquakes, per Mainichi reporting on expert assessments.

Economy-BOJ

BOJ holds rate at 1%, upgrades GDP forecast; yen risks breaching 165 per dollar

The Bank of Japan held its policy rate at 1% at its July meeting and upgraded its GDP forecast even as the yen approached 40-year lows near 163-165 per dollar; analysts at ING and Bloomberg describe the path to the next rate hike as slow. Governor Ueda's upcoming speech is the next signaling opportunity, with multiple sources flagging 165 per dollar as a threshold that could trigger MOF intervention.

So what: The BOJ's hold decision and yen weakness simultaneously pressure PM Takaichi's fiscal expansion plans and the government's market-confidence narrative ahead of the consumption tax cut bill submission.

Tech Times, Bloomberg, ING Think, Japan Times, Jiji Press
China-Japan

China again denies Japan-China foreign minister contact at ASEAN Manila

China's foreign ministry spokesperson reiterated on July 29 that no exchange occurred between Wang Yi and Japan's foreign minister at the ASEAN ministerial in Manila, directly contradicting Tokyo's account for a second consecutive day. China's MOFA separately expressed condolences on the Kumamoto earthquake while also registering strong dissatisfaction over Japan's handling of an SDF member's alleged knife intrusion at the Chinese Embassy.

So what: The contested ASEAN encounter undermines Japan's effort to portray bilateral communication channels as reopened following the ministerial-level blackout triggered by PM Takaichi's Taiwan-related remarks.
