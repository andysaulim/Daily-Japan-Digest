Economy-BOJ

BOJ holds rate at 1% by 8-1 vote; hawkish dissent, yen pass-through warning

The Bank of Japan kept its policy rate at 1% — in line with the previous decision — but the vote shifted to 8-1 from 7-1, with one board member proposing an immediate hike to 1.25%. Governor Ueda stated the impact of foreign exchange on prices is now greater than in the past and that attention on inflation risk is needed more than ever, while separately confirming no major financial disruption from the Kumamoto earthquake.

So what: The widening dissent and Ueda's yen-inflation language tighten the guidance for the next policy meeting and directly bear on the trajectory of yen intervention requirements.

Kumamoto earthquake death toll rises to 38; over 9,200 remain in shelters

The death toll from the M7.1 Kumamoto earthquake rose to 38, with over 9,200 people still in shelters, per multiple sources including Xinhua and the Japan Times. So what: PM Takaichi's scheduled Monday visit to Kumamoto will be the first on-ground appearance since the disaster, with ongoing shelter conditions and heat risk shaping the government's relief timeline.
