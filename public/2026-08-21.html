Russia fires Bastion missiles from Etorofu; Motegi calls military buildup unacceptable

Russia's Pacific Fleet fired Bastion surface-to-ship missiles from Etorofu island on August 21, simulating an attack on enemy vessels with all targets struck approximately 300km away — the first live-fire drill launched from the Japan-claimed island since Putin's August 13 visit. Foreign Minister Motegi issued a formal protest, calling the military buildup on the Northern Territories unacceptable.

So what: The drill escalates the Northern Territories standoff beyond diplomatic protest and signals Russia is using the islands for active military signaling; watch for Tokyo's next response options including additional sanctions or further ambassador-level action.

Pattern: Russia has previously conducted missile drills in the Sea of Okhotsk and around the Kuril chain, but today's launch is the first confirmed Bastion firing from Etorofu itself in the current escalation cycle that began with Putin's August 13 visit.

DPRK

North Korea launches 10-12 SRBMs from Pyongyang toward Sea of Japan hours after Trump announces Kim meeting

North Korea fired approximately 10-12 short-range ballistic missiles from the Pyongyang area toward the Sea of Japan on August 20, a day after Trump announced plans to meet Kim Jong Un and hours after Washington scaled back US-South Korea joint military exercises in an apparent diplomatic overture. Japan's Defense Ministry confirmed the launches, which landed outside Japan's exclusive economic zone; Japan, the US, and South Korea held trilateral information-sharing consultations.

So what: The volley tests whether Trump's renewed diplomatic outreach to Kim can survive simultaneous North Korean provocations; Japan's formal protest and trilateral consultations with Seoul and Washington are the immediate diplomatic track to watch.

Pattern: North Korea has a documented pattern of responding to US-ROK military exercises with missile launches regardless of diplomatic context; the August 1998 Taepodong-1 overflight that catalyzed US-Japan missile-defense cooperation is the foundational precedent in the reference data.
