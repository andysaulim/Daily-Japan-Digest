Japan's National Intelligence Bureau launches July 31; Hara named director

Japan's new National Intelligence Bureau — the country's first modern foreign intelligence organ since World War II — launches July 31, with the government naming Hara as its first director alongside a new oversight body, per Kyodo and Jiji Press. So what: The July 31 launch date is fixed; the first director appointment and the accompanying oversight body structure will determine how quickly the bureau can operationalize its counterintelligence mandate.

Japan identifies large rare-earth deposit in deep-sea mud off remote island

Japanese researchers identified a large share of rare-earth elements — including key minerals — in deep-sea mud collected off a remote island, per Jiji Press and the Japan Times.
