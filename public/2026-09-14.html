Conservative Genta Koja wins Okinawa governorship with record vote, ending 12 years of anti-base governance

Genta Koja defeated incumbent Denny Tamaki in Sunday's Okinawa gubernatorial election, receiving more than 400,000 votes — a prefectural record — with exit surveys showing voters prioritized economic concerns over the US base relocation issue. Koja's victory removes a key local obstacle to the Henoko relocation and Japan's broader southwestern defense buildup near Taiwan.
